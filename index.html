ALUMNI

PORTAL

The LND Alumni Portal is a digital bridge between the alumni and the institution, developed as part of the final year major project by the BCA department of L.N.D. College, Motihari. This portal empowers former students to stay connected with their alma mater, contribute to its growth, and engage with the current student community.

Our mission is to foster a strong, supportive alumni network that encourages collaboration, mentorship, and giving back to the institution that shaped countless futures.
